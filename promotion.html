How the reductions work

Reduced prices are applied automatically at checkout - there is no code to enter and no minimum spend. Each offer applies to one category, and categories are not combined, so the largest single reduction is always the one that applies.

Every reduced piece can still be altered or returned within 30 days, collected from your door at no cost. If a piece sells out during the offer, we will offer an equivalent at the same price.
