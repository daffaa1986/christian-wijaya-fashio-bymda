The atelier

Forty-one hours in every jacket

A jacket is not a pattern and a machine. It is a canvas padded by hand, a lapel rolled with a hot iron, and a pocket bag stitched at a tension that only a maker can feel.

We make in small runs, from materials we can trace. When we cannot trace it, we do not use it.
