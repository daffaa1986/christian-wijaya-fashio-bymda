## Information for participants

### 1. What is this study about?

This is a **university coursework project** studying how usable a website design is. The website being tested is a **prototype** of an online fashion store for a brand called CHRISTIAN WIJAYA (line: CW / PREMIUM), designed to sell in Indonesia.

### 2. Why have I been asked?

You have been invited because you shop online and use a smartphone or computer regularly. We want to understand how the site behaves with people who have not seen it before.

### 3. What will happen if I take part?

- You will be asked to complete **8 short tasks** on the website, such as finding a product and going through a checkout.
- The session takes about **30–40 minutes**.
- You will be asked to **think aloud** — say what you are looking at and what you expect to happen.
- I will not tell you how to do things. If you get stuck, that is useful information, not a failure.
- You may stop at any time, skip any question, or ask me to delete your data — without giving a reason.

### 4. What is recorded?

- **Screen recording** of the website while you use it.
- **Written notes** taken by the observer describing what you did: what you clicked, how long each task took, where you paused or went wrong, and what you said.
- Your **answers to a short questionnaire** at the end (eight rating questions and up to three written comments).

### 5. What is **not** recorded or collected?

- Your name, email address, phone number, student number, employer, or any contact details.
- Any real payment. The site is a **prototype** — no money can be charged, no bank is contacted, and no card details are stored. If the test needs a card number, we use a **dummy number** (`4242 4242 4242 4242`).
- Audio is not retained. Your voice may be captured on the screen recording's audio track only if you agree below; by default the recording is **silent**.
- No photographs of you and no recording of your face.

### 6. How is my data used?

- Everything is stored under a code such as **P03**. The list linking your name to that code is kept separately by the researcher and deleted once the study ends.
- Findings are reported in an academic report. Quotes may appear **anonymously**, attributed only to a participant code.
- Results are published only in aggregate. No single participant is identifiable from the report.
- The anonymised notes, screen recordings and results may be included in the project submission and in the repository where the assignment is marked.

### 7. Are there any risks or benefits?

- There are **no physical, financial or legal risks**. Nothing you do can trigger a real transaction.
- There is no direct benefit to you.
- If any part of the experience is confusing or frustrating, that is exactly what the study is trying to find out — and it improves the design.

### 8. What happens if I change my mind?

Tell me at any point during the session, or afterwards. I will delete your recording, notes and questionnaire responses and remove your data from the results. You do not need to give a reason, and it will not affect your standing in the course.
